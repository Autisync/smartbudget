# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing their own personal finances on a phone — budgeting, tracking
spending, watching savings goals and subscriptions, checking financial health.
They are often anxious or distracted when they open the app (mid-purchase, end
of month, after an alert). They need to understand their money state in seconds
and trust that the numbers are correct. Multi-currency, multi-locale
(en/es/fr/de/pt). iOS-first, also Android.

## Product Purpose

SmartBudget (SmartSpend) is an AI-assisted personal budgeting app. It exists to
turn scattered financial data into a calm, legible picture of "am I OK?" and to
nudge better decisions (burn rate, budget alerts, savings momentum, financial
health score). Success = the user opens it, instantly reads their situation,
trusts it, and acts without friction.

## Brand Personality

Premium-minimal as the floor; bold and serious in the moments that matter.
Three words: **trustworthy, composed, decisive.** Quiet, generous base — then a
confident hero number, a single strong accent, a deliberate motion. Voice is
clear and adult: never cute, never hype, never alarmist. Money is serious, so
the interface is calm; when it speaks up (over budget, at risk) it is direct,
not loud. Reference feel: Monarch / Copilot Money / Apple Wallet for restraint
and trust; a touch of Revolut's confidence in the hero moments — never its
neon.

## Anti-references

- **Generic Material default**: stock M3 widgets, default purple, no point of
  view. Looks like a template.
- **Crypto / neon dark**: glowing gradients, neon-on-black, hype aesthetic.
- **Childish / gamified**: mascots, confetti, badges — destroys financial trust.
- **Cluttered enterprise**: dense gray dashboards, widget soup, no breathing room.

## Design Principles

1. **One source of truth for money colors.** Income, expense, positive,
   negative, and every status are defined once as semantic tokens and never
   hardcoded per screen. Inconsistent greens/reds are the app's biggest tell.
2. **Calm by default, bold on the number that matters.** Most of the screen is
   quiet neutral; visual weight is spent on the one figure the user came for.
3. **Color carries meaning, never decoration.** Danger red means trouble, not
   navigation. Accent = primary action / current state only.
4. **Trust through precision.** Tabular figures, aligned numbers, correct
   contrast, consistent radii and spacing. Sloppiness reads as "is this number
   even right?".
5. **The tool disappears.** Earned familiarity over novelty. Standard
   affordances, consistent component vocabulary screen to screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large/bold text ≥3:1, including text on tinted
  cards and gradients. No gray-on-tint that fails.
- Status must never rely on color alone (pair with icon/label).
- Respect reduced-motion: every animation has a calm/instant alternative.
- Full localization (en/es/fr/de/pt) — no hardcoded user-facing English.
- Dynamic type friendly; numbers must not clip at large sizes.
