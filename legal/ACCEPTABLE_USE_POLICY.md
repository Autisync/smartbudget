# Acceptable Use Policy

**App:** SmartSpend
**Operator:** Firmeza Consulting cc
**Effective Date:** 05 April 2026
**Last Updated:** 05 April 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets standards of conduct required of all SmartSpend users
worldwide. It applies to use of the App, connected backend services, exported data, and
communications with us. The AUP is incorporated into our Terms of Use.

## 2. Permitted Use

You may use SmartSpend solely for lawful, personal, non-commercial personal finance management
consistent with these Legal Documents. Commercial use (resale, redistribution, or use in
services offered to third parties) requires our prior written consent.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

### 3.1 Unlawful or Harmful Activity

- Violate any applicable law, regulation, or third-party rights;
- Process, store, or transmit data relating to illegal financial activity including money
  laundering, fraud, or tax evasion;
- Impersonate any person or entity, or misrepresent your identity or affiliation;
- Harass, threaten, or harm any person.

### 3.2 Security and System Integrity

- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to any backend system, database, or account;
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of any system or network without our express written
  authorization;
- Introduce malicious code, viruses, ransomware, or harmful components into the App or backend;
- Circumvent, disable, or tamper with authentication mechanisms, rate-limiting controls,
  biometric or PIN protections, or any other security feature;
- Attempt to intercept, decode, or compromise JWT tokens or encryption key material;
- Use automated scripts or bots to make excessive requests to backend API endpoints in a manner
  that degrades service availability.

### 3.3 Reverse Engineering and Intellectual Property

- Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the App except to the extent expressly permitted
  by mandatory applicable law;
- Copy, reproduce, or distribute the App or backend services without authorization;
- Remove, alter, or obscure any proprietary notices or copyright statements.

### 3.4 Data Misuse

- Use export or share features to distribute personal or financial data of third parties
  without their consent;
- Aggregate exported App data with external sources in a manner that creates unauthorized
  profiles of third parties;
- Use exchange-rate data accessed through the App for commercial currency dealing, financial
  instrument trading, or any purpose where rate accuracy is material.

### 3.5 Misrepresentation

- Submit false, misleading, or fraudulent information during registration or password reset;
- Create multiple accounts to evade suspension or enforcement actions.

## 4. Enforcement

4.1 We reserve the right, in our sole but reasonable discretion, to:

- Issue a written warning;
- Temporarily suspend backend access;
- Permanently terminate your account;
- Report conduct to relevant law enforcement or regulatory authorities in applicable jurisdictions;
- Pursue civil or criminal remedies available under applicable law.

4.2 Enforcement actions will be proportionate to the nature and severity of the violation. We
will consider context and provide notice where feasible and not contrary to security or legal
requirements.

4.3 We are not obligated to monitor all user activity, but we reserve the right to investigate
suspected violations.

## 5. Reporting Violations

Contact support@smaartspend.com to report AUP violations. For security vulnerabilities, follow our
Security Disclosure Policy.

## 6. No Limitation of Applicable Law

Nothing in this AUP limits our rights or your obligations under applicable law, including
computer fraud, data protection, or financial regulation statutes of any jurisdiction.
