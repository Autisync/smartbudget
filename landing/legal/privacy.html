1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Firmeza Consulting cc ("we," "us," "our") processes personal data when you use the SmartSpend mobile application ("App"). It applies to:

  • Data processed entirely on your device (local-first mode);
  • Data processed by our optional backend authentication service when you create an account; and
  • Data incidentally processed when you use third-party integrations or OS-level features.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or behavioural-tracking services.

SmartSpend is available worldwide. This Policy applies to all users regardless of country of residence. We have designed our data practices to meet the requirements of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), UK GDPR, Brazil's Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados (LGPD), and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)/CPRA as high-water marks, providing protections applicable globally. Where your local law grants greater rights, we will honour them.

2. Data Controller and Sub-Processor Roles

2.1 Controller. Firmeza Consulting cc is the data controller for personal data processed via the optional backend service (account credentials, session metadata).

2.2 Local device data. Financial and preference data stored exclusively on your device within the App's local encrypted database is under your own control. Firmeza Consulting cc acts as a software provider with respect to locally stored financial data, and does not routinely access it.

2.3 Sub-processors. We engage the following key sub-processors who act on our instructions:

Sub-Processor Role Data Location
Autisync Database management, backend hosting, email delivery infrastructure Hetzner servers, Germany (EU)

All sub-processors are bound by data processing agreements requiring GDPR-compliant processing.

3.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored in the App's local encrypted database on your device. It does not leave your device unless you explicitly export or share it:

  • Transactions: amount, currency, category, date, notes, recurrence metadata, account association, timestamps.
  • Accounts: name, type, balance, currency.
  • Budgets: category, limit amounts, alert thresholds, periods, date ranges.
  • Savings goals: title, target and saved amounts, deadlines, priority, auto-allocation settings.
  • Subscriptions: name, description, amount, billing cycle, payment method label, website (URL), importance, active status, timestamps.
  • Security settings: session timeout preferences, biometric and PIN enable flags, lockout configuration.
  • Local auth logs: device ID, authentication type and status, timestamps; IP address and user agent are optional fields populated only if technically available on the device.
  • In-app notifications: title, body, category, read status, timestamp.

Biometric data: The App uses OS-level biometric APIs (e.g., Face ID, fingerprint) for local
unlock decisions only. We do not store, access, or transmit biometric templates or biometric data of any kind. All matching is performed exclusively by your device's operating system.

3.3 Data in OS Secure Storage

Certain sensitive values are stored in your device's OS secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), isolated to the App's sandbox:

  • Authentication tokens (access and refresh tokens, JSON format).
  • Biometric preference flags and last authentication timestamp.
  • Legal/onboarding acceptance flags.
  • Theme, locale, currency, and other user preferences.
  • Encryption key material for the local database encryption service.

3.4 Analytics and Usage Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, telemetry, advertising, or behavioural-tracking services. We do not collect screen-view, feature-usage, session, or pseudonymous event data, and we do not embed any analytics SDK in the App. The aggregations and charts you see in the App (spending summaries, budget progress, insights) are computed locally on your device and are not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Because no analytics is collected, there is nothing to opt out of in this respect.

3.4a Crash and Error Reports

When the App crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a crash report to our error-monitoring provider, Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), with data stored in the European Union (Frankfurt). A report contains the error and where it happened in the code, the App version, the device model and operating-system version, and, if you are signed in, the random ID of your account. It never contains your transactions, balances, budgets, savings goals, notes, name or email address, and the App never records your screen. We use these reports only to find and fix faults (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)) and keep them for up to 90 days.

3.5 Exchange-Rate Lookups

When you use currency conversion features, the App contacts third-party exchange-rate APIs (ExchangeRate-API, Open ER API, or Frankfurter/ECB). These requests include currency pair identifiers and standard network metadata (IP address in transit). All exchange-rate data displayed in the App is labelled "for reference only." See Third-Party Services Notice.

3.6 Automatically Collected Technical Data (Backend)

When you use the optional backend, we or our infrastructure providers may automatically log:

  • IP address (for security and rate-limiting);
  • Device name and platform (stored in session records for session management);
  • Timestamps of authentication events;
  • HTTP request metadata necessary for service operation.

3.7 Push Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you enable push notifications, the App registers with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver remote alerts (for example, security or billing notices). For this purpose:

  • A device push token (an opaque identifier issued by FCM/Apple Push Notification service) is generated and may be transmitted to our backend so we can address notifications to your device. The token does not contain your name, email, or any financial data.
  • On Android 13+ and iOS, the operating system asks for your permission before any notification is shown; you can revoke this at any time in your device settings or in Settings > Notifications within the App.
  • Local (on-device) notifications — budget alerts, savings nudges, and similar reminders — are generated entirely on your device and are not transmitted to us or to Google.

Google Firebase acts as a processor for push delivery. See the Third-Party Services Notice. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent (you opt in by enabling notifications).

3.8 Social Sign-In (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, the respective provider returns a signed identity token from which we read your email address and, where available, your first/last name solely to create or match your account. We do not receive your social-account password. Apple's "Hide My Email" relay addresses are supported. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — contract performance.

3.9 Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect: raw biometric data; bank account or card numbers; Social Security, tax identification, or national identity numbers; health or medical data; precise geolocation; advertising identifiers; or financial transaction data from your bank or payment provider.

8. Data Retention

Please refer to our Data Retention Policy for full schedules. In summary:

  • Backend account data: Retained while your account is active and for 30 days following confirmed account deletion.
  • Auth sessions: Active until expiry or revocation; revoked session records retained 90 days for security audit, then purged.
  • Local device data: Remains on your device under your control. Uninstalling the App removes this data (subject to OS backup behaviour outside our control).
  • Password reset / verification tokens: Deleted on use or TTL expiry.
  • Analytics data: None collected, so none retained.
  • Exchange-rate query data: Not retained by us; third-party providers' own policies apply.

10. Security

We implement technical and organisational security measures appropriate to the nature and sensitivity of the data, including:

  • Argon2 password hashing;
  • JWT access and refresh tokens with rotation, revocation, and configurable TTLs;
  • OS secure storage (Keychain/Keystore) for sensitive client-side values;
  • Local database encryption with key material in OS secure storage;
  • Authentication endpoint rate-limiting;
  • Email verification and single-use token flows with TTL enforcement;
  • EU-based server infrastructure (Hetzner, Germany) with physical and logical security controls.

We cannot guarantee absolute security. Please refer to our Security Disclosure Policy.
